Adventure · Snow · Mountains

Manali

Manali is one of Himachal's best-known mountain destinations, surrounded by forested slopes, the Beas River and dramatic Himalayan scenery. It is popular with couples, families, friends and adventure lovers.

What can you explore?

  • Hadimba Devi Temple and Old Manali
  • Solang Valley for mountain activities
  • Atal Tunnel and Sissu on suitable routes
  • Local cafés, markets and riverside scenery

Suggested duration

3–4 days for a relaxed trip, depending on your sightseeing plans and road conditions.

Shimla

Shimla combines colonial-era architecture, lively streets, forested hills and beautiful viewpoints. It is a popular choice for first-time visitors, families and couples who want a classic hill-station holiday.

What can you explore?

  • The Ridge and Christ Church area
  • Mall Road for shopping and cafés
  • Kufri for mountain scenery and activities
  • Jakhoo Temple and surrounding viewpoints

Suggested duration

2–3 days, with additional time if you want nearby excursions.

Enquire About Shimla
Nature · Riverside · Slow Travel

Tirthan Valley

Tirthan Valley is known for its peaceful surroundings, clear mountain streams, traditional villages and forested landscapes. It is ideal for travellers who want to slow down, enjoy nature and spend time away from crowded towns.

What can you explore?

  • Tirthan River and riverside spots
  • Gushaini village and local scenery
  • Nature walks and village experiences
  • Great Himalayan National Park region

Suggested duration

2–3 days for a relaxed valley stay. Park access and hikes may require permits or local guidance.

Enquire About Tirthan
Cafés · River · Backpacking

Kasol

Kasol sits beside the Parvati River and is known for its mountain views, relaxed café culture and access to nearby villages. It attracts backpackers, groups of friends and travellers seeking a mix of nature and local culture.

What can you explore?

  • Parvati River and scenic walking areas
  • Kasol market and cafés
  • Nearby Chalal village walking route
  • Manikaran Sahib and its hot springs

Suggested duration

2–3 days, depending on whether you add nearby village excursions.

Enquire About Kasol
Village Life · Forests · Relaxation

Jibhi

Jibhi is a charming mountain village with forested hills, wooden-style architecture, streams and a quiet atmosphere. It suits couples, small groups and travellers looking for a slower, nature-focused holiday.

What can you explore?

  • Jibhi Waterfall and forest paths
  • Traditional village surroundings
  • Nearby Jalori Pass, when accessible
  • Serolsar Lake hike, conditions permitting

Suggested duration

2–3 days for the village and nearby attractions. Check weather before high-altitude excursions.

Enquire About Jibhi
High Mountains · Snow · Photography

Sissu

Sissu is a scenic village in the Lahaul region, reached through the Manali–Leh highway and Atal Tunnel route when conditions permit. It is known for stark mountain landscapes, open valleys and striking seasonal scenery.

What can you explore?

  • Sissu village and surrounding valley
  • Sissu Waterfall, depending on season
  • Mountain photography and scenic stops
  • Local landscapes and high-altitude views

Suggested duration

A day trip from Manali or an overnight stay, depending on road and weather conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which destinations can I enquire about?

You can enquire about Manali, Shimla, Kasol, Jibhi, Tirthan Valley and Sissu. Tell us your travel dates and preferred route to discuss a suitable plan.

Can I customise my tour package?

You can share your interests, travel dates, group size and budget. Available options and the final itinerary can then be discussed before confirmation.

Are hotel and cab services included in every package?

Not necessarily. Inclusions depend on the specific offer. Always confirm accommodation, vehicle, meals, sightseeing, entry fees and other costs before booking.

When is the best time to visit Himachal?

Spring and summer are popular for comfortable sightseeing, while autumn offers crisp views. Winter may bring snow to some destinations. Weather, road access and high-altitude routes vary by season.

How do I get package prices?

Contact us on WhatsApp with your dates, number of guests, nights and preferred destination. Prices depend on availability, season, hotel category and transport requirements.
